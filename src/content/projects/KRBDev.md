KRB Foundation is an organization that offers additional education for undeserved communities. They needed a **informational business brochure website** that reflected that explains their orgnaization and allows those interested to reach out and volunteer.


 ## What I did

**Website**

-   **Astro** – for fast, lightweight performance
    
-   **Tailwind CSS** – for clean, responsive design
    
-   **JavaScript + Alpine.js** – to add dynamic features and interactivity
    
-   **Netlify** – for secure, free hosting with blazing speed
    


**CMS Integration**
- To make updates easy, I connected the site to **Pages CMS**. This allows the client to:

-   Add new team members
    
-   Update basic information
    
-   Manage who reaches out to them 
    



## Results


KRB Foundation has a educational and useful online brand that fully explains their mission and allows for user engagement!

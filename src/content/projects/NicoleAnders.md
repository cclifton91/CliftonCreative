Nicole Anders is an event coordinator who specializes in planning and executing high-end events. She needed a **two page website** that would showcase her work and attract new clients.

[Link To Website](https://www.nicoleandersevents.com/)

 ## What I did

**Design**
The client wanted a fun, lively website that caught attention but portrayed professionalism. 

-   A fun, elegant color scheme
    
-   Great photos provided by the client
    
-   Videos that would catch a users attention
    

**Website**

-   **Astro** – for fast, lightweight performance
    
-   **Tailwind CSS** – for clean, responsive design
    
-   **JavaScript + Alpine.js** – to add dynamic features and interactivity
    
-   **Netlify** – for secure, free hosting with blazing speed
    






## Results


A beautiful and lively website that shows off her event building skills and her personality.
